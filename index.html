READING THE DATA

From public studies to useful comparisons.

Start with the right biological question.

Retina Spectacle brings public studies cited by the diabetic-retinopathy review together with newly discovered, supported retinal studies. Conditions, species and tissues remain distinct. Diabetes without retinopathy is not a healthy control; oxygen-induced retinopathy is not diabetes.

Choose groups, then choose cells.

Recorded conditions pool matching sample libraries within one study. You can instead group by sample, provisional cell type, cluster, cell type × condition, custom sample assignments or two cell selections. Filters intersect: a cell must pass the sample, tissue, cell type, cluster and population filters. Include or exclude plot groups to control every plot and export.

Read distributions and gene panels.

Box-and-whisker plots include zero-expression cells and draw every selected cell when the points option is enabled. The box spans the first and third quartiles, the line is the median and whiskers extend to observed values within 1.5× the interquartile range. Dot area encodes the fraction of detected cells; dot color encodes the group mean. Violin plots show smoothed histograms of the same values.

Compare without overstating the evidence.

Selected-gene comparisons run in your browser using a two-sided, asymptotic Mann–Whitney test with tie correction and a 0.5 continuity correction. Each group needs at least three non-overlapping cells. Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment applies only to the selected genes. Fold change is log₂((mean expm1(expression) in A + 1) / (mean expm1(expression) in B + 1)). These cell-level tests do not account for donor replication, batch effects or covariates. They are exploratory, not donor-level differential expression.

Keep provenance visible.

Each study records its source files, processing and sample labels. Raw counts are filtered, normalized to counts per 10,000 and log-transformed before embeddings and clustering. Deposited normalized data retain their documented scale. Marker-based cell type suggestions are provisional and need expert review. Original study design and condition evidence are available below every workspace.

Public data, automatically maintained.

Free scheduled jobs check supported repositories every 30 minutes. Completed data versions stay available while updates process. Restricted-access and unsupported raw-only sources are listed separately. A new version is offered when ready; an open workspace keeps its existing version so the comparison does not change underneath you.
